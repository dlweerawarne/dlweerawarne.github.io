Biography, education, research background and interdisciplinary interests.

Profile

I am Professor in Physics at the Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, University of Colombo, Sri Lanka.

My research connects fundamental physics with practical engineering challenges, especially flexible and hybrid electronics, low-cost fabrication, laser-assisted materials processing and instrumentation. Earlier work in nonlinear optics and ultrafast laser propagation continues to inform my approach to experimentally grounded problem-solving.

Alongside research and teaching, I contribute to university administration, academic quality assurance and the development of learning opportunities beyond conventional campus-based programmes. I also write Sinhala poetry, exploring themes that intersect with scientific curiosity.
